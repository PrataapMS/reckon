# Roadmap

Reckon grows in phases. Each phase ends with a working app that is more useful than the last. Progress is tracked on the [GitHub project](https://github.com/users/PrataapMS/projects/3).

## Done

### Phase 0: Foundations
A deployable skeleton that reports its own health: append-only forecast ledger, work queue, CI, nightly backups and live data-source checks.

### Phase 1: Daily Brief MVP
Every morning, weather and air-quality questions for your places with honest probabilities. Every evening, a reveal of what happened. Delivered in the PWA and on Telegram.

### Phase 2: World Pulse and Evidence
Questions about money, earthquakes, space weather, software releases, service incidents and the news. Headlines behind each claim, a first scoreboard (Brier scores and a reliability diagram) and Web Push.

## Next

### Phase 3: Honest Scoreboard and the Habit
See what Reckon is genuinely good at, and have a reason to open it daily.
- Trust labels backed by statistics on every probability
- Calibration views and per-forecaster champions
- Streaks, milestones and reveal animations (all optional)
- "Call it": make your own call and compare against Reckon
- Weekly report with a "surprise of the week"
- Custom questions and data export

### Phase 4: Learning Reckon
- A blended forecaster tuned by backtesting
- An evidence-reading AI forecaster with strict guardrails
- Crowd forecasts as a baseline

### Phase 5: Life Integration
- Opt-in, read-only Todoist and Google Calendar questions
- Personal results kept in their own bucket, never mixed with world statistics

### Phase 6: Decisions
- Action cards only for question types with proven skill, each showing the record that justifies it
